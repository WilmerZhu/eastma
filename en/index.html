What is compared

The quantitative results use SinoScope1.0, EARA2024 and FWEA23. All three are recent adjoint full-waveform models with overlapping East Asian coverage. Comparisons are made in the laterally demeaned perturbation domain so that different 1-D references do not dominate the scores. Forward runs share CRUST1.0 above Moho.

Independent scoring against observed waveforms, model fusion and adjoint inversion are still in progress and are not conclusions of this site.

Working conclusions (mantle only)

  • The three models are closest in the lithosphere (about 80–200 km). Vs SSIM is 0.55–0.72 there, then falls through the crust, transition zone and below 660 km.
  • On a common 1.0° grid, EARA2024 and FWEA23 are about 1.7 times more similar to each other (Vs depth-mean SSIM 0.49) than either is to SinoScope1.0 (about 0.28).
  • Each model yields 30 velocity clusters. High-velocity clusters at 100–200 km and in the transition zone coincide with Slab2 for EARA2024 and FWEA23; SinoScope1.0 keeps the same first-order provinces with softer boundaries.
  • Synthetics of FWEA23 and EARA2024 stay close to the author reference; SinoScope1.0 departs more in the 20–40 s surface-wave band. Because crust is fixed to CRUST1.0, this statement applies only below Moho.

Region and data

The study region covers East Asia and the adjacent western Pacific, including the Tibetan Plateau, North and Northeast China, South China, Southeast Asia, and the Japan–Izu–Bonin–Mariana subduction system.

The waveform test set uses 30 events from GCMT / GCMT3D. Public FDSN stations are screened for three-component completeness, record length of at least 1800 s, and SNR ≥ 4 in 20–100 s. After screening, 135 stations enter the forward geometry (417 unique stations; 2161 event–station pairs; about 69% pass rate).

Public FDSN coverage is sparse inside continental China relative to the ~814 provincial stations used by FWEA23. That gap must not be read as a model ranking.

Model library

Twenty-four published lithosphere–upper-mantle models (2018–2026) were collected from the literature and IRIS EMC. Only the three quasi-continental FWI models share a common volume for a single similarity matrix. Regional and local models are kept as qualitative references.

Structural similarity

SSIM (Wang et al., 2004) is computed layer by layer on a common 1.0° grid in the perturbation field δlnV. A phase-randomised surrogate gives a noise floor of about 0.05–0.07. One-dimensional SSIM of the horizontal-mean profiles is 0.90–0.99, so the disagreement lives in lateral pattern, not in the background gradient.

Velocity clusters

A Gaussian mixture is fit independently in four depth bands (crust / lithospheric mantle / transition zone / lower mantle) on (δlnVp, δlnVs). The component counts are a tectonic prior, K = 10 / 10 / 5 / 5, not an argmin of BIC. Labels are sorted by mean δlnVs inside each band. Geology and Slab2 are overlaid only; no NMI or purity score is computed.

Forward waveforms

SPECFEM3D Globe is run on one mesh for all three models, with identical CMTSOLUTION and STATIONS. Assignment order in the solver writes 3-D crust after the mantle. CRUST1.0 is therefore imposed above Moho in every run, so synthetic differences isolate mantle structure. The band is 20–40 s and 40–120 s. Present scores compare synthetics with the author reference (AuthorRef), not with observations.

Methods in brief

Standardisation

Models are rewritten as CF-compliant NetCDF with dimensions (latitude, longitude, depth). Speeds are in km/s, depth is positive downward from the surface. Names: vp, vs, vpv, vph, vsv, vsh, rho, eta. The three core models already have native Vp and Vs; Brocher (2005) and mantle scaling are not applied to them.

Perturbation and common grid

δlnV = (V − V̄(z)) / V̄(z), with V̄ the area-weighted horizontal mean. All pairs use the intersection domain (about 10°–55°N, 80°–150°E) on a 1.0° grid and a common validity mask (layer coverage about 0.90). A finer native grid would make pair-wise SSIM values incomparable.

SSIM

Local statistics use a Gaussian window of σ = 2.0°. Layers are thickness-weighted. The structure term is reported separately so that a pure amplitude rescale is not confused with a pattern change. The null model keeps the power spectrum and randomises phase (20 draws, seed 42).

Clustering

Features are z-scored δlnVp and δlnVs. Bands follow Moho, 410 km and 660 km. Full-covariance GMMs are fit by EM. K is held fixed across models. BIC is diagnostic only.

Forward tests

SPECFEM3D Globe writes crust after mantle. Enabling a crustal model therefore overwrites Moho-up values. The runs share CRUST1.0 above Moho on purpose: residuals can be attributed to the mantle. The period floor is 20 s. Windows target SNR > 4 and CC > 0.7. Observed-waveform χT / χA / χF with geographic and category weights (Zhou et al., 2021) are reserved for a later paper.
